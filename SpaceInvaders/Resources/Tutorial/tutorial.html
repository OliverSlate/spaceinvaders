You!

This is your ship.
You start with 3 health points, with a maximum of 5
(increased with power-ups)
- Getting hit by an enemy projectile costs 1HP;
- Getting hit by a special projectile costs 3HP;
- Colliding with an enemy kills both of you;
You may pick up shields to mitigate damage.
Don't let your health go down to 0!

Controls

Use the WSAD controls to move around

Press SPACE to shoot

Power Ups

A power up might spawn every 5 seconds in the middle of the board.

  • HP Pickup - restores 1 HP
  • Multiplier Pickup - doubles score for 5 seconds
  • Shield Pickup - gives 1 Shield
  • Bullet Pickup - quadruples attack speed for 5 seconds

Touching a power up adds 5 seconds to the timer,
ergo possibly infinite power ups.

score

Defeating an enemy increases the multiplier by 0.02;

Taking damage resets the multiplier.

waves

There are 3 kinds of waves:

Normal wave - 8-64 random enemies. Each enemy's speed slightly varies.

Swarm - A row of Cappy's and a row of Tanks. Occurs every 6th non-boss wave with a maximum of 32 enemies.

debug options

You can also choose any of the following debug modifiers to change your gameplay:

  • Force Boss Wave - every wave will have a boss.
  • Invincible - You can't die. Colliding with enemies has no effect.
  • Super Speed - move 4 times faster.
  • Fast Attack - You shoot a laser beam of bullets. (100 rps to be exact)
